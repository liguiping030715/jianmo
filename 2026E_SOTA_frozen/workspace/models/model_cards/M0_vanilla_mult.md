# M0 — vanilla minimal CPU MulT-style native backbone

Hypothesis H11: directional text-to-audio and text-to-vision cross attention over the native unaligned A2 grids can be a viable Q2 predictor. It consumes frozen BERT 50×768, audio 500×74, vision 500×35 and produces polarity logits and intensity. Audio and vision remain on their native grids; text is the query grid. The simplest credible comparator is the frozen RU native pooling model; frozen R1 aligned is contextual only. The structural motivation is asynchronous modality streams. The pilot can reject viability if clean and paired-gap metrics miss the frozen competitive bounds or resources fail. This architecture has no claim of recovered time alignment, official missingness, or causal explanations.

Exact architecture, optimization, selection, resource and promotion rules were frozen in [q2_bridge_plan.md](../../experiments/q2_bridge_plan.md) before training. M0 sees trusted P padding only; it has no learned observation-state adapter. No current-problem answer or architecture detail is copied from historical papers. Inputs are A2 train/valid v2 only; frozen local BERT states need no external sentiment data. See [M1 card](M1_mult_observation_adapter.md) for the isolated adapter comparison.
