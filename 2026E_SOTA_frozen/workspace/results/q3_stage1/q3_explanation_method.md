# Q3 Stage 1 — frozen explanation method (specified before scoring)

Scope: A2 unaligned **valid only** (728 rows), the previously frozen M0 seed2718 checkpoint, frozen v2 inputs, pinned tokenizer/BERT. This is a model-response validation, not a real-world causal analysis. No A2 test or Attachments 3/4 enter this stage.

## Targets

For each sample, compute full logits `z`, predicted class `c*=argmax(z)`, and regression output `y`. For every declared intervention `I`, score classification `Delta_cls(I)=z_full[c*]-z_I[c*]` (signed) and regression `Delta_reg(I)=abs(y_full-y_I)` (nonnegative). Use the **original full** `c*` throughout. Keep the two targets and their modality rankings separate. No attention weight is an explanation score.

## Operators and fixed design

1. **Modality donor replacement.** Generate one deterministic, no-self-donor permutation manifest with NumPy seed 1729 over the 728 valid rows, independently for text, audio and vision. Swap the entire donor tensor and its native frozen P/O masks. Text donor states come through the pinned tokenizer/BERT path, checked against the frozen valid cache. Also evaluate audio+vision replacement with the same two donor maps. This tests dependence on sample-specific content; donor responses are reference-dependent.
2. **Local text deletion.** On the recipient's original A2 `text_bert` triplet, replace a candidate content span by token ID 100 (`[UNK]`), preserve `[CLS]`, `[SEP]`, padding, token types and attention mask, then rerun the **same frozen BERT**. Candidate lengths are 1, 3 and 5 WordPiece tokens where possible. At each length, take at most 12 uniformly spaced start indices over eligible content. Record half-open indices and both WordPiece and decoded phrase. `[UNK]` replacement is a declared model intervention, not verified source omission.
3. **Local audio/vision deletion.** Eligible native positions have frozen `P=1,O=1`. Candidate support-window widths are `ceil(1%,5%,10% × eligible count)`, capped at `eligible count-1` to leave a key; take at most 12 uniformly spaced rank starts at each width. Temporarily set only the chosen positions' **model-input** P to 0, leaving feature values, frozen on-disk P/O, and unknown positions unchanged. Store native half-open bounds, selected support count, relative native index and support fractions. No feature-index-to-seconds conversion.
4. **Faithfulness.** Use the 3-token text and 5%-support audio/vision candidate families. Rank single-interval responses per sample and target. Select three pairwise nonoverlapping intervals for top and bottom; choose three from the same candidate family with a fixed sample/modality/target random stream. Apply each set **jointly**, giving equal token/support counts within a sample. Compare top, random and bottom joint deletion responses separately for classification and regression. Retention keeps only selected eligible positions: other text content becomes `[UNK]` and BERT is rerun; other known A/V support is excluded, while unresolved native support remains untouched. Retention is reported as a separate response, not treated as a deletion effect. Skip a sample/target when three equal-size disjoint groups cannot be formed. Rank-then-joint-test is still in-sample and may favor top by construction; interpret it as an operator consistency check, not external validity.
5. **Stability.** On a fixed, evenly spread subset of up to 128 valid rows, use the bottom 3-token/5%-support candidate as a small input perturbation. Keep cases with unchanged class and `|Δy|≤0.1`. Re-score remaining same-scale candidates on that perturbed input. Report top-1 agreement and top-3 Jaccard separately for classification/regression; absent candidates or prediction-changing perturbations are counted and not silently dropped.

The local candidate grids and selection rules are fixed here before responses are inspected. A classification deletion may have a negative signed response. Native zeros are not translated into missing labels; these temporary support masks do not modify cleaning outputs.

## Review rule

For each modality and target, report the aggregate mean top/random/bottom joint-deletion response, ordering, paired top-minus-random and random-minus-bottom bootstrap 95% intervals (1,000 fixed-seed resamples), eligible count, and retention/stability. A modality/target passes the **operator consistency check** only if both paired mean differences are positive and both intervals exclude zero. Stage 1 method is accepted only if text passes for at least one target, at least one A/V modality passes for at least one target, and no technical-contract violation occurs. Otherwise report `Q3_EXPLANATION_METHOD_NOT_FAITHFUL`. This does not certify causal importance or exact source timestamps.

Artifacts are generated by `workspace/experiments/q3_stage1_validate.py` with frozen hashes and one deterministic manifest recorded in `q3_faithfulness.json`.
