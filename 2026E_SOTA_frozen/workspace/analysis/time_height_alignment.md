# Stage 2 Time and Height Alignment: Question 1 Rehearsal Subset

## Alignment policy

This report quantifies exact support and nearest-grid differences only. It does not select a time tolerance, interpolation method, aggregation rule, vertical datum, or turbulence indicator.

The exact calculations are generated by `workspace/analysis/scripts/run_stage2_audit.py` and recorded in:

- `workspace/analysis/audit_artifacts/time_alignment.csv`
- `workspace/analysis/audit_artifacts/height_alignment.csv`
- `workspace/analysis/audit_artifacts/audit_summary.json`

## Time support by sensor

### Station A

- Microwave: 16 unique cycles at 2-minute spacing from 00:00 through 00:30.
- Profiler: six files at 6-minute spacing from 00:00 through 00:30.
- Exact common timestamps: all six profiler times.
- Exact common microwave cycles: six, because no A timestamp is duplicated.

### Station B

- Microwave: 18 cycles at 12 unique timestamp labels from 00:02 through 00:30.
- Profiler: six files at 00:00, 00:06, 00:12, 00:18, 00:24, and 00:30.
- Exact common timestamps: 00:06, 00:12, 00:18, 00:24, and 00:30.
- Exact common microwave cycles: six because 00:24 has two distinct microwave cycles.
- The 00:00 profiler file has no exact microwave match. Its nearest microwave label is 00:02, two minutes away, and that label contains two cycles.

## Height-grid support

### Microwave grid

Both microwave files use the same 83 labeled heights from 0.000 to 10.000 km:

- 0.000–0.500 km at 0.025 km increments;
- 0.550–2.000 km at 0.050 km increments;
- 2.250–10.000 km at 0.250 km increments.

### Profiler grid

All profiler files use the same 62 integer vertical-coordinate tokens from 100 to 11,380. Adjacent differences are 60 for 12 transitions, 120 for 10 transitions, and 240 for 39 transitions.

The problem statement describes profiler spacing in metres, so the audit also performs a conditional numerical comparison in which microwave kilometers are multiplied by 1,000 and compared with profiler tokens. The profiler files themselves do not state the unit or whether the coordinate is AGL or MSL. Therefore, all matched-height results below are conditional on metre correspondence and do not resolve vertical datum.

## What can be aligned safely now

- Timestamp labels can be compared for exact string-equivalent times.
- The presence and count of cycles at a time can be retained, including station B duplicates.
- Profiler slash-filled rows can define unavailable vertical positions.
- Identical grid tokens can be identified numerically under the explicitly stated metre-correspondence condition.

## What cannot be aligned safely yet

- Station B microwave and profiler measurements cannot be treated as spatially collocated because their metadata positions are separated by 32,739.660 m.
- A time tolerance cannot be selected from the files, so station B 00:00 cannot automatically use 00:02.
- A duplicate-selection or duplicate-aggregation rule cannot be selected for station B.
- Heights cannot be converted between AGL and MSL because the datum and site-elevation meanings are unknown.
- Interpolation, smoothing, temporal averaging, and vertical resampling have not been justified or performed.
- Even an exact time/height label match does not identify comparable physical variables while `q11`–`q14` and `W1`–`W6` remain undocumented.

No aligned modeling table has been created because doing so would require at least one unresolved semantic, spatial, temporal, or vertical decision.
