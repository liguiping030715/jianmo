# Q2 frozen-checkpoint robustness figures

The three Q2 factorial figures answer distinct descriptive questions using `workspace/results/q2_evidence/factorial_cell_metrics.csv`, generated by `workspace/experiments/q2_factorial_evidence.py`. All show A2 unaligned valid performance change from paired clean predictions on the same complete-case cohort. Error bars are sample SD across three **frozen checkpoints/seeds**, not population confidence intervals. The factorial mask manifest and numeric summaries are saved alongside the figures. These are synthetic local-gap responses, not certified Attachment 3 missingness or physical seconds.

| Figure | Question | X axis | Y axes | Comparison | Supported takeaway | Paper section |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `q2_missing_type_performance.png` | How does affected modality combination change performance? | text, audio, vision, audio+vision | macro-F1 degradation; MAE increase | M0 vs M1, mean±seed SD, averaged over position and ratio | Relative sensitivity to the four declared synthetic missing-modality types | Q2 robustness |
| `q2_missing_position_performance.png` | How does native-index position change performance? | early, middle, late | same | M0 vs M1, averaged over type and ratio | Position sensitivity under a balanced native-index intervention grid | Q2 robustness |
| `q2_missing_ratio_duration_performance.png` | How does requested relative duration change performance? | 10%, 20%, 30% of eligible support | same | M0 vs M1, averaged over type and position | Relative gap-length response; native length distribution is in the evidence table | Q2 robustness |
