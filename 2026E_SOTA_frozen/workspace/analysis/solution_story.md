# Stage 1.5 proposed solution story for architecture review

The official tasks naturally share a **source-linked temporal observation**: sample ID, modality sequence, valid-position mask, local-availability mask, and a mapping (possibly uncertain) to source time/text/frame. Q1 builds and verifies that object for 100 raw clips. Q2 consumes the equivalent Attachment 2 interface, adds local reliability state and yields polarity/intensity under gaps. Q3 uses the same predictive interface on complete inputs and derives modality and local evidence through declared interventions, mapping the selected positions back to Attachment 4 media. This is an architecture hypothesis, not a trained model. Attachment 1 features are not silently substituted for Attachment 2 training features.

The repaired full-library retrieval supports the **representation and validation sequence**, especially 2024E source-to-coordinate verification, 2025D variable-source reliability with a weak-teacher warning, and 2025E split-safe preprocessing and explanation limits. It does not transfer their physical equations, model names or scores. Expanded SOTA coverage adds native/asynchronous versus hard/soft/text-anchored timing; direct masking versus distillation, uncertainty, experts, reconstruction and invariance; and ablation, gradients, perturbations, counterfactuals, localization and faithfulness. None forces a final network.

`raw text/audio/video → modality-specific temporal features → documented coordinate + valid/missing masks → observed reliability → fused predictive state → polarity/intensity → intervention-based modality contribution and local source evidence`.

Evidence categories: three modalities, local gaps, two targets, explanation fields and split discipline come from the **official problem**; exact array shapes and length fields are **official interface descriptions**; reliability/distillation/dynamic fusion are **recent-literature mechanisms** subject to pilots; alignment-first, baseline-first and claim-matched validation are **historical modeling patterns**. No chosen solver or final network follows from those sources alone.

Version choice remains open. Aligned 50 offers simpler shared positions, lower compute, and direct feature-position comparison, but may discard acoustic/visual detail and obscure where local gaps occur. Unaligned 50/500 retains more native timing and potentially finer evidence intervals, but increases compute and needs a defensible cross-time map. Both require true valid lengths and mask semantics; neither guarantees index-to-video seconds. Stage 2/pilot must compare temporal information loss, localization quality, missing detection, accuracy/robustness, cost and reproducibility under the same version across all split/special interfaces.

Architecture review gate: confirm whether one timeline/provenance structure is technically recoverable from official media/PKL; if not, retain modality-native intervals and disclose localization resolution. Also establish whether a complete-input teacher is reliable before any distillation, and define modality contribution through a fixed intervention rule before reporting it. Do not start Stage 2 automatically.
