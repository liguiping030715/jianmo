# Paper Writing

## Evidence-first rule

No numerical claim enters the paper unless traceable to saved evidence or a verified source.

## Abstract

Prefer a compressed solution chain:

- one short sentence on the core problem/difficulty,
- for each sub-question: abstraction/method + key verified result,
- final validation/robustness or practical conclusion.

Avoid generic background padding.
Do not announce an algorithm without explaining the problem-specific role it plays.

## Question sections

Prefer:

1. problem analysis,
2. why this representation/model fits,
3. mathematical formulation,
4. solver/implementation,
5. results,
6. validation/discussion,
7. direct answer and handoff to the next question.

Do not write long textbook introductions to standard algorithms.

## Narrative

The paper should tell one solution story.
Explicitly show when Question 2 uses Question 1 outputs, etc.

## Final checks

- every requested deliverable answered,
- every number traceable,
- every table/figure cited,
- every reference verified,
- notation consistent,
- no copied historical prose,
- no unsupported innovation claim.
